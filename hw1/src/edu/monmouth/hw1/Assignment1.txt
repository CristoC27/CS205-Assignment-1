Q: What is meant by self-referential class? Give a simple example.

A: A self-referential class makes a reference to another item of the same class. Nodes in a linked list are an example, each node holds a reference to the next node in the list.


Q: Your assignment is to create a program that houses passengers on a boat. Boats will range from personal
boats (just 1 or 2 passengers) to cruise ships (hundreds or thousands of passengers). Would you use an
array or a linked list? Explain your answer.

A: I would use a linked list because i do not know what each boat will be able to hold. The linked list can change in size as needed by each boat's capacity.


Q: The passengers are stored in an unordered list. You are given a passenger to find in your array or list.
What is the Big O of this algorithm? Explain your answer.

A: Searching a linked list for the passanger has a time complexity of O(n) because we do not know where the passanger is in the list.

Q: What is the Big O of your size() method? Explain your answer.

A: The size() method has a Big O of n because the method iterates through the entire list to calculate its size.


Q: What is the Big O of your removeFirst() method? Explain your answer.

A: The removeFirst() method has a Big O of 1 because the list already knows what the head node is.
